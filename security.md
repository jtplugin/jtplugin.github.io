# Security Policy — JT Plugin Development

**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

This policy covers the plugins for Autodesk® Fusion® developed by Giovanni Tommasi — JT Plugin Development: JT 3D Slicer, JT Magic Revolve, JT Bevel Gears, JT Box Joint and JT Flat Layout.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Send security vulnerabilities by email to **[info@jtplugin.it](mailto:info@jtplugin.it)**, with "Security" in the subject.

Please **do not** report vulnerabilities in public GitHub issues: those are for usage questions and bugs.

A useful report includes the plugin and its version, your operating system and Fusion version, what an attacker could do, and the steps to reproduce it.

---

## What We Commit To

- **We confirm receipt within 7 days.**
- We tell you whether we confirm the issue, which versions it affects and how serious we consider it.
- **We aim to make a fix available within 90 days** of your report. Updates reach users through the Autodesk Marketplace, whose review time we do not control: if it delays the fix, we tell you and publish an advisory with the measures users can take in the meantime.
- We tell you when the fix is out and, if you wish, credit you in the advisory. We do not run a bug bounty program.
- We will not take legal action against anyone who reports a vulnerability in good faith and follows this policy.

## What We Ask of You

- Keep the details confidential until a fix is available, or for 90 days from your report, whichever comes first.
- Do not access, modify or delete data that is not yours, and do not disrupt other users or our license service.

---

## Security Advisories

When a vulnerability is fixed, we publish an advisory in the **Security advisories** section of the affected plugin's help page and in the release notes of the new version on the Autodesk Marketplace. The advisory describes the issue, the affected and fixed versions, its impact and what to do.

Security updates are free for all license holders. Each plugin's help page states until when it receives security updates.

If a vulnerability is being actively exploited, we also inform affected users directly and report it to the competent authorities as required by EU law.
